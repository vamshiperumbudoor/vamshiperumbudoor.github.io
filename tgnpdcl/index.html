What each charge means

Energy charges · ₹1,148

Amount for the electricity units consumed during the billing period. Applicable slab rates and adjustments determine the final charge.

Fixed / customer charge · ₹50

A fixed service charge that may apply based on the connection category and sanctioned load.

కనెక్షన్ కేటగిరీ లేదా మంజూరైన లోడ్ ఆధారంగా విధించే స్థిర ఛార్జీ.

Fuel / power purchase adjustment

Where applicable, this reflects notified adjustments to power procurement costs. Check the bill for the exact line item.

వర్తించే సందర్భాల్లో విద్యుత్ కొనుగోలు ఖర్చుల సర్దుబాటు ఛార్జీ.

Electricity duty & taxes

Government levies shown separately on the bill where applicable.

వర్తించే ప్రభుత్వ పన్నులు లేదా విద్యుత్ సుంకాలు.

Arrears / late payment

Previous unpaid balance or late fee, if any. Your sample account currently shows no arrears.

మునుపటి బకాయిలు లేదా ఆలస్య రుసుము ఉంటే ఇక్కడ చూపబడుతుంది.

Credits / adjustments

Common questions

How is my bill calculated?

Your bill can include energy charges based on applicable tariff slabs, fixed charges, adjustments, duty, taxes, credits and arrears.

Why is my bill higher this month?

Compare units, billing days, meter readings, tariff changes and any one-time adjustments or previous balances.

Can I see appliance-wise usage?

Only with suitable sub-metering or connected smart devices. A conventional utility meter reports total premises consumption.

Is this connected to TGNPDCL?

No. This is an independent concept demo using fictional sample data. Official integration requires authorization and utility APIs.
